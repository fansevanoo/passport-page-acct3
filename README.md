A fictional passport spread for the Republic of Mirell (Mirellska Republika), an invented volcanic island nation that does not exist. Its holder is Ana Kovač, a volcano observatory warden, and every page of the document is shaped by the island's one rule: the crater road opens only when the air is safe to breathe.

The stamps on the visas page tell two round trips — to Dravna in August 2023 and to Kelm in March 2025 — each stamped in matched entry and exit pairs, all after the passport's issue date.

The program in this repository (`main.ts`) copies the finished spread, `entry.png`, into the output directory.
